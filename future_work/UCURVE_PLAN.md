# PLAN — the fixed-thrust throttle curve (pre-registered 2026-08-07)

**Status: PLANNED, committed before any of its runs launched.** Predictions
below were generated by `model/ucurve_targeting.py` from the committed
calibration only; this document is the pre-registration the three
`capstone.ucurve_*` stages test.

## What it measures, and why the frontier does not already measure it

The committed voltage frontier (100 / 200 / 300 V) holds **perveance** fixed
at the validated I/I_CL = 1.46 and lets thrust vary (~V² along the path). It
measures the *envelope boundary* — where the device can operate. It does not
measure the *cost surface inside the envelope*: what power a **fixed thrust
demand** costs as the supply voltage drops.

That fixed-thrust slice is where the flight rule lives. The §7b servo
(`SCALING_LAWS.md`) commands `V_opt = ctrl_factor · φ` with
`ctrl_factor = (2α+1)/α ≈ 3.12` — derived from the calibrated laws, i.e. from
the **H1 branch below, which no committed run has tested off the frontier
path**. If beam optics degrade as the commanded current climbs past the
validated perveance, the true specific-power valley sits at a *higher* V than
the H1 laws claim, and below some voltage the demand has no operating point
at all. The servo's safety clamp (`V_HW floor = 100 V`) is currently a design
assumption; these stages measure the physics that justifies (or corrects) it.

## The three stages

Fixed demand **F = 13.65 nN** — the 200 V anchor's measured thrust, making
the anchor itself the fourth (committed) point of the curve. Commanded
currents solve the calibrated laws at that demand (`model/ucurve_targeting.py`;
constants α = 0.8931, c_F = 3.2675, κ = 0.8063, βA = 2.507 cm²):

| stage | V | i_beam | I/I_CL | over-ceiling | expected steps (~800 ns) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `capstone.ucurve_valley` | 125 V | 0.464 mA | 4.0 | 2.7× | ~128 k |
| `capstone.ucurve_left_arm` | 92.4 V | 0.601 mA | 8.2 | 5.6× | ~111 k |
| `capstone.ucurve_floor` | 78 V | 0.840 mA | 14.7 | 10.1× | ~103 k |

Same deck as `capstone.low_power` in every other respect: geometry, plasma
row, grid (dx 0.15 mm), ppc 16/16, reservoir, charge pump, t_end 800 ns,
seed 42. The two lower voltages sit deliberately **below the 100 V hardware
floor** — they probe the boundary that motivates the floor.

## Pre-registered competing hypotheses

**H1 — frontier optimism (the calibrated laws, escape held at the
frontier-path value).** Beam optics stay benign despite the perveance
excursion; escape stays ≥ 96 %; delivered thrust lands on the demand.
Numeric predictions (escape clamped at 96.12 % below 100 V):

| V | φ [V] | χ | KE [eV] | P [mW] | P/F [mW/nN] |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 125 | 24.5 | 215 | 81.1 | 58.0 | 4.25 |
| 92.4 | 32.5 | 286 | 48.3 | 55.5 | 4.07 |
| 78 | 47.3 | 416 | 24.8 | 65.5 | 4.80 |
| 200 (anchor) | 17.0 meas. | 149 | 147.5 meas. | 68.4 | 5.01 meas. |

Under H1 the specific-power valley sits near **V ≈ 95 V** (the self-consistent
`V = 3.12·φ` point at this demand): P/F at 92.4 V comes out *below* P/F at
125 V, and even 78 V (φ brushing the 50 V benign limit, χ = 416 — far outside
the measured χ ≤ 319 envelope) still delivers the demand.

**H2 — the perveance tax.** The commanded current exceeds the only validated
emission ceiling by 2.7× / 5.6× / 10.1×. Three committed pieces of in-repo
evidence say that is not free:

- `emitter.holed_anode` scenario B: at 79 % of planar I_CL the beam already
  blows open radially and transmission collapses until the aperture is doubled.
- The killed first slender attempt (`SLENDER_BODY_PLAN.md` amendment): a
  commanded current far over the gap's ceiling self-scraped 91 % of the beam
  inside the can — the float never charged past 0.3 V.
- `capstone.low_power`: even at the *validated* perveance, escape already
  degrades toward low V (96.1 % at 100 V vs 98.4–99.0 % at 200–300 V).

Under H2, escape collapses with over-ceiling ratio, delivered thrust falls
**short** of the 13.65 nN demand (the shortfall growing as V drops), the true
P/F at the delivered thrust *rises* on the left arm — putting the measured
valley at or right of 125 V, not 95 V — and at 78 V the demand has **no
operating point**: the beam fails by optics before the float binds
(low φ, massive self-scrape), or the equilibrium that does form sits at a
thrust far below demand.

**Discrimination.** H1 and H2 disagree on the *sign* of
P/F(92.4) − P/F(125), on escape at all three points, and qualitatively on
what 78 V even converges to. One run per point decides.

**Either way, the collection law wins.** Every converged run yields an
(I_esc, φ) equilibrium pair at χ beyond the current fit range — extending the
in-repo α fit from 3 points on one slice to up to 6 points on two slices.

## Acceptance

φ, escape, and delivered thrust are **the measurement** at these operating
points, so (per the `capstone.exploratory_axes.v1` precedent) they are
reported, not required. Required gates are the trust set only — steady
current balance, momentum bound, sheath/plume containment, and both
ledger-vs-dump cross-checks, tolerances byte-identical to the frontier
policies. Exception: `capstone.ucurve_floor` (78 V) also demotes
*steady current balance* to reported — H2 predicts that run may never reach
a steady state at all, and a policy must not gate away its own hypothesis.
Each stage carries its own `acceptance.yaml` (policy ids
`capstone.ucurve_valley.v1`, `capstone.ucurve_left_arm.v1`,
`capstone.ucurve_floor.v1`).

A FAILED verdict on the floor stage under those trust gates (e.g. containment
lost) is a bad run and gets rerun; a PASS with collapsed escape/thrust is the
H2 *finding*, not a failure.

## Cost

Measured 0.154 s/step on the §1 machine (`SETUP.md`) at this domain size:
~5.5 h + ~4.8 h + ~4.4 h ≈ **15 GPU-hours**, run strictly sequentially
(one arena fits the 12 GiB card).

## What it opens

- The flight rule's valley location and no-go boundary become measured
  quantities; `SCALING_LAWS.md` §7 and the paper's throttle section cite
  these stages instead of model extrapolation.
- The α fit gains high-χ points off the frontier slice (χ 215–416 commanded).
- The mission sweeps' low-V rows move from flagged extrapolation toward
  measured envelope (or gain a measured no-go wall).

## AMENDMENT — 2026-08-08: all three runs complete, discrimination resolved

All three stages ran to 800 ns on the committed configs (seed 42, git clean)
and **PASSed every required trust gate**; verdicts and evidence are promoted
under each stage's `reference_results/`. Measured, with specific power at
**delivered** thrust:

| V | escape | φ_body (V) | delivered F (nN) | P/F (mW/nN) | late dφ/dt |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 78 | **57.43 %** | 23.84 | 10.38 (−24 %) | 6.31 | +23.6 mV/ns |
| 92.4 | **79.95 %** | 23.72 | 11.59 (−15 %) | 4.79 | +24.7 mV/ns |
| 125 | **93.78 %** | 21.25 | 13.09 (−4.1 %) | **4.43** | +18.4 mV/ns |
| 200 (anchor) | 98.44 % | 16.98 | 13.65 | 5.01 | +16.5 mV/ns |

**H2 (the perveance tax) wins the discrimination.** The pre-registered
discriminator — the sign of P/F(92.4) − P/F(125) — is **positive**
(4.79 > 4.43): the valley sits at ~125 V, not at H1's untaxed ~95 V. Escape
collapses monotonically with over-ceiling ratio (93.8 → 79.9 → 57.4 % at
2.7× → 5.6× → 10.1×), exactly the H2 mechanism; delivered thrust falls
correspondingly short of the demand.

**The floor's wall has a different shape than either branch predicted.** At
78 V a steady equilibrium *does* form (current balance 0.035 — H2's
no-steady-state variant did not materialize), but it is starved: −24 % thrust
at 10× the validated ceiling, with F_net/F_beam = 0.89 (the self-scraped
beam loads the body nearly as hard as the exhaust pushes it). Meeting the
demand would take still more current at still lower escape — the demand has
**no operating point** at 78 V, which is H2's operative claim and the wall
the flight rule's voltage floor exists to avoid. H1's 47.3 V float never
materialized (measured 23.84 V).

**Where the tax lives.** `emitter.voltage_bracket` scenario C (the same
92.4 V command in the clean isolated-gun geometry) transmitted 0.9999 — no
generic gun-optics loss. The escape collapse measured here is therefore
attributable to the can's own gap and lid/collection physics, not to beam
formation.

**The collection law survives the slice change.** Each converged run's
(I_esc, φ) pair joins the fit: all six committed equilibria — two slices of
the (V, I) plane, escape fractions from 57 to 99 % — fit
`I_esc = βA·j_the·(1+χ)^α` with α_all = 0.922, βA = 2.27 cm², residuals
≤ 9.3 % (`model/minimal_model.py --calibrate`).

**Flight-rule consequence** (recorded in `model/MODEL.md` §2): the untaxed
closed form `V_opt = ((2α+1)/α)·φ` under-shoots the measured optimum
(V/φ = 5.9 at the valley) and is demoted to a hard lower bound; the valley
is shallow rightward (+13 % power at 200 V), so overshoot is cheap and
undershoot is what the curve punishes.
